Research project with **Wadhwani AI** (Jul 2024 – Dec 2024) on automatically segmenting long educational videos into coherent scenes.

## Highlights

- Implemented **LGSS (CVPR'20)** and **NeighborNet** using multimodal features, **Bi-LSTMs**, and **ResNet50 embeddings**.
- Processed the **AVLectures** and **MovieNet** datasets, replicated published baselines, and ran cross-domain evaluations on the CwS benchmark.
- Achieved **mIoU = 0.894**, **mAP = 13.5%**, and **F1 = 0.435** through domain-specific fine-tuning and graph refinements.
