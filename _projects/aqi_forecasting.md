Ongoing research at **MADHAV Labs, IIT Kanpur** (Jan 2025 – Present) on forecasting air quality with graph neural networks.

## Highlights

- Built **spatial-temporal graphs** linking ERA5 reanalysis grids and AQI monitoring stations across **133 locations**.
- Implemented an encoder–decoder architecture using **GATConv, GCNConv, and SAGEConv**; achieved **RMSE 33.64**.
- Addressed missing sensor data using interpolation and imputation strategies.
