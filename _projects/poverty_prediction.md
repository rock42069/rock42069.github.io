Research at **CDIS Labs, IIT Kanpur** (Sep 2023 – Apr 2024) on predicting poverty levels from open geospatial data.

## Highlights

- Combined **VIIRS nightlight data, Mapbox satellite imagery, and OpenStreetMap features**; fine-tuned **VGG16** for feature extraction.
- Trained **XGBoost + SVM** classifiers to predict income levels for **7M+ families in Haryana**.
